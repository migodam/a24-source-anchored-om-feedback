Do not average or sum duplicate rows as if they were independent reusable caches; use job receipts and attempt attribution. Preparation rows currently lack explicit job IDs.

Disposition: current failure retention, observed recovery accounting, information-flow call sites, and HOLD/quality-target gate behavior are mechanically consistent. Publication path sanitization and the latent recovery/provenance/accounting gaps remain for parent review. No numerical or scientific status was promoted by this audit.
